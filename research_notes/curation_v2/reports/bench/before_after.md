# Curation v2 retrieval benchmark

- Same `test_blind` split is used for before and after.
- Thresholds are fit independently on `dev_gate` for each corpus and prediction variant; `test_blind` is report-only.
- The acceptance target is at most 3% wrong among answered and coverage above 36.5%.
- Wilson intervals are 95% row-level intervals. One deterministic pass was run; they do not represent run-to-run variance.
- LAPTOP latency is warm retrieval + Laya ONNX per test row. Query embedding and model/index startup are excluded.
- Historical 1,790-row evaluation: legacy, not blind; it selects no configuration and contributes no threshold.
- Phone RAM/latency: not measured, no adb device.
