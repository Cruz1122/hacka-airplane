### Parques Nacionales Naturales, ficha Los Nevados

- URL: https://www.parquesnacionales.gov.co/nuestros-parques/parque-nacional-natural-los-nevados/
- HTML `article:modified_time`: 2026-10-31T00:11:49+00:00 (read from the saved page markup).
- Header: departments Caldas, Quindío, Risaralda, Tolima; extensión total 61.388,24 ha; región Andes Occidentales; declaratoria 30/04/1974.
- Three main snow summits of one volcanic complex: Ruiz (Kumanday), Santa Isabel (Poleka Kasue), Tolima (Dulima).
- Glaciers occupy 1.82% and correspond to those three volcanoes. That percentage is ecosystem cover; the geography answers do not reuse it.
- Location paragraph: cordillera Central, between Magdalena and Cauca, "61.410 hectáreas", ten municipalities: Santa Rosa de Cabal, Pereira, Villamaría, Murillo, Anzoátegui, Ibagué, Santa Isabel, Casabianca, Herveo and Salento.
- Four ecotourism sectors: Brisas-Kumanday, Cisne-Poleka Kasue, Laguna del Otún, Quindío-Dulima.
- Brisas induction center about 4,100 m; road through Arenales to Valle del Silencio or Valle de las Tumbas, near Ruiz.
- Cisne-Poleka Kasue: views of Ruiz, Santa Isabel and paramillo del Cisne; trail toward Laguna Verde; El Cisne visitor center about 4,100 m.
- Laguna del Otún: largest lake in the park, surrounded by paramillos del Quindío and Santa Rosa; Potosí cabin about 3,920 m.
- Quindío-Dulima: surroundings of Nevado del Tolima.
- Multimedia: northern volcanic complex formed by Ruiz or Kumanday, craters La Olleta and La Piraña, Santa Isabel or Poleka Kasue, Tolima or Dulima, and paramillos del Cisne, Santa Rosa de Cabal and Quindío.
- Otún wetlands inside the park were declared a Ramsar site.
- Conservation values include upper basins of Chinchiná and Otún.
- 2025: Nevado del Ruiz declared Patrimonio Natural de la Nación. Cultural etymologies on the same page are left to history.

### SGC fact sheet, Nevado del Ruiz, September 2024

- URL: https://www2.sgc.gov.co/Temas-destacados/Documents/Hoja-informativa-volcan-Nevado-de-Ruiz-SGC.pdf
- Cordillera Central, border of Tolima and Caldas. Height 5,321 m. Age about 66,000 years.
- Composite or stratovolcano. Active crater named Arenas.
- Pre-Columbian names Cumanday, Tabuchía and Tama are recorded here; etymology is not reused as geography.
- Part of a volcanic complex with 1.8 million years of history, accompanied by inactive volcanoes La Olleta, La Piraña and Nereidas.
- Faults highlighted: Palestina and Termales-Villamaría.
- Inside PNN Los Nevados.
- IUGS (UNESCO) since 2022: one of 100 geological heritage sites of international relevance.
- Glacier cap and hot springs are mentioned. Hot-spring coordinates were not in the readable text, so no thermal entry was written.

### SGC annual bulletin, northern volcanic segment, activity of 2023 (document year 2024)

- URL: https://www2.sgc.gov.co/Publicaciones/Informes%20tcnicos/Informe_de_actividad_volcanica_segmento_norte_2023.pdf
- Sections read: segment membership, Ruiz generalities, geological periods, and the 2023 alert summary. The 2023 alert colors are not copied into visitor answers.
- Ruiz: composite polygenetic volcano, 5,321 m, crater Arenas, maximum diameter 800 m and depth 200 m in this bulletin.
- Coordinates 4°53'43'' N, 75°19'21'' W. About 140 km WNW of Bogotá and 28 km SE of Manizales.
- Glacier cover "aproximadamente 8 km² (IDEAM 2021)" appears in this bulletin. Geography does not repeat glacier areas; flora owns retreat figures. The 2024 IDEAM page later gives 7.17 km², so the bulletin figure is stale.
- Complex includes inactive La Olleta, Nereidas, Piraña and smaller centers.
- Current edifice ages cited as 66 ± 5.6 ka and 26.8 ± 5.6 ka; ancestral Ruiz between 1.8 and 0.97 Ma.
- Monitored northern segment list, not labeled as a north-south ranking: San Diego, Romeral, Cerro Bravo, Nevado del Ruiz, Paramillo del Cisne, Nevado de Santa Isabel, Paramillo de Santa Rosa, Paramillo del Quindío, Complejo Volcánico Cerro España, Nevado del Tolima and Cerro Machín.
- Figure caption read: lava dome Paramillo del Cisne and dome Morro Negro.

### SGC crater morphology note, 2020-01-31

- URL: https://www2.sgc.gov.co/Noticias/Paginas/El-Nuevo-Domo-de-Lava-del-Volcan-Nevado-del-Ruiz-y-la-Geomorfologia-Actual-del-Crater-Arenas-2020.aspx
- Summit 5,321 m. Arenas irregular and elongate: major diameter 980 m SW-NE, minor 900 m SE-NW, depth about 300 m, as described for that date.
- A lava dome on the crater floor was estimated in January 2020 at 173 m across, 60 m high and about 1,500,000 m³. Those dome dimensions are not used in visitor answers because they describe a 2020 snapshot of an active crater.

### IDEAM glacier pages, read 2026-11-21

No separate page-publication date was visible. Data tables run through 2024.

- Ruiz: https://www.ideam.gov.co/nuestra-entidad/ecosistemas-e-informacion-ambiental/glaciares/nevado-del-ruiz
  - Municipalities Villamaría (Caldas), Casabianca and Murillo (Tolima), inside the park.
  - About 4°53' N, 75°19' W. Single summit above 5,321 m.
  - Cites Ordóñez et al. 2020 for the 980 m by 900 m, about 300 m crater.
  - Drainage table includes Azufrado, Gualí, Chinchiná, Lagunilla and Recio, toward Magdalena-Cauca.
  - 2024 glacier area 7.17 ± 0.30 km² is not copied into geography answers.
- Santa Isabel: https://www.ideam.gov.co/nuestra-entidad/ecosistemas-e-informacion-ambiental/glaciares/nevado-santa-isabel
  - Inside the park. Dome complex, three summits aligned north-south: Sur 4,935 m, Central 4,923 m, Norte (Hongo) 4,903 m, from SRTM 12.5 (IGAC) and Topcon GPS.
  - Lowest glacier in the country at 4,903 m. Conejeras ice extinct between December 2023 and February 2024.
  - Early 2024 ice 0.16 ± 0.01 km² is not copied into geography answers. The qualitative fact used is that remaining ice is fragmented.
  - IDEAM also names an ice remnant "Laguna Verde" on the eastern slope. That name is not treated as proof that the remnant and the park's Laguna Verde trail are the same point.
- Tolima: https://www.ideam.gov.co/nuestra-entidad/ecosistemas-e-informacion-ambiental/glaciares/nevado-del-tolima
  - Tolima department, municipalities Ibagué and Anzoátegui, inside the park.
  - About 4°39' N, 75°19' W. Single summit at 5,215 m from SRTM 12.5 (IGAC).
  - Conical stratovolcano. Crater and fumaroles at the mountaineering point called El Oído.
  - Drains toward Coello and Totare.
  - 2024 glacier area 0.45 ± 0.04 km² is not copied into geography answers.

### Ramsar information sheet CO1781

- URL: https://rsis.ramsar.org/RISapp/files/RISrep/CO1781RIS.pdf
- Sheet filled or updated 2006-07-17.
- Complex inside PNN Los Nevados, Risaralda, Pereira and Santa Rosa de Cabal.
- Complex area 6,578.5 ha; altitude 3,300 to 4,850 m. These figures describe the 2006 complex, not the open water of the lake and not a 2026 survey.
- Laguna del Otún called the most important wetland of the complex.
- Río Otún born in the lagoon at 4,000 m, municipality of Pereira, Cauca basin.
- Formation: Santa Isabel lava dammed a glacial valley (IGAC, 1993, cited on the sheet). Seventeen smaller wetlands nearby, also on that 2006 sheet.
