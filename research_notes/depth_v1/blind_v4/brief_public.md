# Brief for test writers (public information only)

You are writing realistic messages that visitors send to an offline chat assistant inside a mobile app
about **Parque Nacional Natural Los Nevados** (Colombia). You have never seen the assistant's content.
Write what real people would type. Do not try to guess what the assistant knows.

## The park, from public tourist knowledge
- A national natural park in Colombia's Cordillera Central, shared by Caldas, Risaralda, Quindío and
  Tolima, near the cities of Manizales, Pereira, Armenia and Ibagué and towns such as Salento, Murillo,
  Villamaría and Santa Rosa de Cabal.
- High mountains: Nevado del Ruiz (an active volcano, monitored by the Servicio Geológico Colombiano),
  Nevado de Santa Isabel, Nevado del Tolima, the Cisne and Quindío paramillos. Glaciers are shrinking.
- Landscapes: high Andean forest, páramo with frailejones, super-páramo, lakes such as Laguna del Otún,
  wetlands, wax palms in the valleys near Cocora.
- Wildlife people ask about: spectacled (Andean) bear, mountain tapir, condor, hummingbirds, parrots,
  frogs, deer, pumas.
- Visits: entry sectors with gates, booking ahead, entrance fees (different for Colombians, foreigners,
  students, children, seniors), registered guides/operators, hiking routes and summits, camping,
  photography, drones, pets, fishing, fires, waste.
- Practicalities: getting there by bus, jeep, own car; roads; opening hours and closures; weather (cold,
  rain, fog, sun at altitude); what to wear and pack; food, water, toilets, lodging, phone signal.
- Health and safety: altitude sickness, hypothermia, getting lost, accidents, volcanic alerts and ash,
  emergency numbers, what to do if something happens.
- Culture and history: indigenous names of the volcanoes, the 1985 Armero tragedy, coffee region towns.

## Personas (rotate among them)
1. Laura, 28, from Bogotá, first trip to the páramo, worried about the cold and altitude.
2. Don Jairo, 67, from Pereira, going with his grandchildren, asks short practical questions.
3. Mateo, 19, student from Manizales, budget backpacker, writes with typos and slang ("parce", "pa").
4. Sophie, 34, French tourist, writes in English or simple Spanish.
5. Andrés, 41, mountaineer, wants summits, routes, permits and technical detail.
6. Valentina, 25, birdwatcher and photographer, asks about species and best spots.
7. Familia Gómez (mother writing), from Ibagué, with a dog and two kids, planning a weekend.
8. Camilo, 30, tour driver from Salento, asks about roads, schedules, rules for groups.

## General style rules
- Spanish of Colombia mostly (tú/usted/vos mixed), ~15 % English, some typos and missing accents,
  some fragments of 1–4 words, some long messages with context ("voy con mi mamá de 70...").
- No numbering, no quotes around the text, one message per row.
